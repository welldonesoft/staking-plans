# Staking Plans Reference

A comprehensive, AI-friendly catalog of betting staking plans — covering Back, Lay, and Each-Way strategies with a focus on Betfair Exchange betting.

## What Are Staking Plans?

A **staking plan** is a money management strategy that determines how much to stake on each bet. Unlike selection systems (which pick *what* to bet on), staking plans control *how much* to bet — managing risk, compounding growth, and protecting your bankroll.

## Key Concepts

- **Flat Staking** — Same stake every bet. Safest, simplest.
- **Proportional Staking** — Stake a % of bankroll. Compounds growth, limits ruin.
- **Positive Progression** — Increase stakes after wins. Ride hot streaks.
- **Negative Progression** — Increase stakes after losses. Recover losses faster (higher risk).
- **Recovery Staking** — Aggressively chase losses to return to profit. Very high risk.
- **Target-Based** — Aim for a specific profit target, adjusting stakes accordingly.

## Betfair Exchange Notes

When using staking plans on Betfair Exchange:

- **Commission** (typically 2–5%) reduces net profit — plans like Kelly should be commission-adjusted.
- **Lay betting** has variable liability — ensure your bankroll can cover worst-case liability.
- **Back odds** may differ from lay odds — factor in the spread.
- **Liquidity** matters — large stakes may not be matched at desired odds.

## Risk Warning

Staking plans do not create profit from losing selections. No money management system can turn a negative expected value into a positive one. Always gamble responsibly and never stake more than you can afford to lose.
