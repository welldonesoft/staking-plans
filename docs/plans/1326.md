# 1-3-2-6 Staking System

## Overview

A positive progression system where stakes follow the sequence 1, 3, 2, 6 units. Reset to 1 unit after any loss or completing the sequence. The idea is to capitalize on winning streaks while risking only your initial unit.

## How It Works

The sequence represents multiples of your base unit:

| Step | Stake (units) | If Win | If Lose |
|------|---------------|--------|---------|
| 1 | 1 | Profit: +1 unit → next step | Loss: −1 unit → reset |
| 2 | 3 | Profit: +3 → total +4 → next step | Loss: −3 → net −2 → reset |
| 3 | 2 | Profit: +2 → total +6 → next step | Loss: −2 → net +2 → reset |
| 4 | 6 | Profit: +6 → total +12 → reset | Loss: −6 → net 0 → reset |

**Key insight:** You only risk your initial 1 unit. After step 1 wins, the system's profits fund the remaining stakes. Maximum downside is 2 units (lose at step 2). Maximum upside is 12 units (complete the full sequence).

## Parameters

| Parameter | Type | Default | Description |
|-----------|------|---------|-------------|
| `unit` | number | 1 | Base unit size |

## Risk Assessment

- **Risk Level:** Medium
- **Max Drawdown:** 2 units per cycle (lose at step 2 after winning step 1)
- **Recommended Bankroll:** 20+ units
- **Ruin Risk:** Low — stakes are capped at 6 units and only escalate after wins

## When to Use

- You want to ride winning streaks without chasing losses
- Your selections occasionally hit 2–4 consecutive wins
- You prefer a disciplined, mechanical approach

## When to Avoid

- Very low strike-rate systems — you'll rarely progress past step 1–2
- You want to maximize every winning bet (you sometimes "give back" profits, e.g., step 4 loss = net zero)

- Works for both back and lay betting

- Commission reduces net profit from the 12-unit max — at 5% commission, max net ≈ 11.4 units
